<h2>The problem</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>I frequently get refurbished computers from Recircuit, a storefront on Amazon.  They offer good computers at excellent prices but occasionally the onboard Intel Graphics connection gets stuck displaying only one resolution, usually 1366x768.  No idea why; it just gets stuck.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2>How to fix</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Enough back story, Here's how I fix it:<br>-Right click on the desktop and go to "Graphic Properties...'<br>     <em>This should bring up a window asking if you want to go to the basic, advanced, or wizard set up.  if you see this window, select advanced mode.  If you don't see this window someone probably selected the 'do not show this window' checkbox.  If you're not in advanced mode there is a drop down menu on the right hand side, above the section that says display, of the window that will give you access.</em><br>-Under the Display section of the right hand menu, select "Custom Resolutions".<br>-You'll get a pop up window warning you that if you change the resolution to something that the onboard graphics / card can't handle, it can cause software and / or hardware issues.  Click OK.<br>-Custom Resolutions will appear and put your desired resolutions in the width and height areas.<br>     <em>Common resolutions are: 1024x768 / 1152x864 / 1280x960 / 1440x900 / 1680x1050 / 1920x1080</em><br>-Next will be the refresh rate. Most monitors have a refresh rate of 60hz.  <br>-After that click 'Add' and restart your computer.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2>Why does this happen?</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Short answer, I wish I knew.<br>Long answer, sometimes drivers fail or don't get installed correctly and fall back to alternative drivers to at least display the OS.  I have yet to find to a specific reason for why do happens, but it's rare enough that I don't consider it to big the biggest of issues but can be annoying when it does show up and gives the impression that you have no other options.  Hopefully this addition to your crime fighting arsenal will prove to be useful.</p>
