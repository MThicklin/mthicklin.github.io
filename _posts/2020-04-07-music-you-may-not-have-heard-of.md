<p>In our "Instant Information" world, content gets cranked out faster than it can be consumed (Quick Note: I hate that we use phrases like "consume" and "content" when talking about entertainment. It makes everything sound generic and sterile). Because of this, a lot of music gets missed that we might like. </p>

<p>Typically, I stick to whatever is on my iPod; but no person is an island... Both literally and musically. </p>

<p>So I make it a point expand myself musically by either digging deeper into unfamiliar genres,  listening to the radio (for just a bit, I mean I still have some self respect...) Or occasionally, and may my ancestors forgive me, buy the latest "Now that's what I call Music" compilations. </p>

<p><em>Yeah, it's real cute to make fun of popular stuff. Like "We get it, you're SO above such things" but those albums aren't that bad and tend to pretty much cover the bases of stuff I missed; It's popular for a reason</em>.</p>

<p>I want to dive into a couple of songs that I have come across in my travels and maybe help others find a new song they might like.</p>
<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3><a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=H-wm0EdoeN8" target="_blank">Elevator Operator</a></h3>


<!-- wp:list -->
<ul><li> Artist(s): Courtney Barnett / Punk Rock /  Australian</li></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->
<p>Songs that tell a story in a unique way is an automatic fast track to the like list.  Elevator Operator is a great example of this.  Australian musician Courtney Barnett tells the story of a gentleman fed up with his job and life in general.  He decides to venture to the top of a tall building to view the city and meets a lady on the way up who thinks he going to jump.  Courtney Barnett's great deadpan, spoken-word style plays beautifully with her witty lyrics.  In the video, it's structured in a way that it didn't have choruses.  However, when the song is played on Spotify, it has a chorus; but only at the end.    </p>
<!-- wp:quote -->
<blockquote class="wp-block-quote"><p>All the people look like ants from up here<br>And the wind's the only traffic you can hear<br>He said All I ever wanted to be<br>Was an elevator operator, can you help me please?</p><cite>Courtney Barnett - Elevator Operator</cite></blockquote>
<!-- /wp:quote -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3><a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=65u_tbL19tA" target="_blank">Northern Lights</a> / <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e37Ri_5xY5U" target="_blank">Southern Cross</a></h3>


<!-- wp:list -->
<ul><li> Artist(s): 403 Forbiddena /  Melodic Power Speed Metal Rock  /  Japan</li></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->
<p>Long ago, in 2006, Flash was king and we were but lowly subjects to it's rule. But things worked out pretty well.  We got some amazing animations set to equally incredible music.  403 Forbiddena is best described as "Melodic Power Speed Metal Rock" and, dear reader, they earn every one of the words in that description. These 2 songs are densely packed epics with heavy drums, rhythm guitars and occasional organ pieces that, can be over powering to the first time listener, but is well worth the time for an adventurous listener.</p>

<blockquote class="wp-block-quote"><p>The Northern Lights - It's the bright rainbow<br>The Northern Lights - It shines everything<br>The Northern Lights - In the starly night<br>Eternal glory! The Northern Lights...</p><cite>403 Forbiddena - Northern Lights</cite></blockquote>
<!-- /wp:quote -->

<!-- wp:quote -->
<blockquote class="wp-block-quote"><p>The Southern cross is twinkling and watching us.<br>Far beyond, so far away from us.<br>The Southern Cross is a holy light.<br>We are the crusaders of the Southern Cross.</p><cite>403 Forbiddena - Southern Cross</cite></blockquote>

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3><a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=q_ZUhJ33X80" target="_blank">Happy Birthday to Me</a></h3>


<!-- wp:list -->
<ul><li> Artist(s): Bulldog Mansion / K-Pop Rock / Korea</li></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->
<p>In 2016, a federal court declared the song "Happy Birthday" was finally placed in the public domain. </p>

<p>But the jokes on them, as my birthday song has been Bulldog Mansion - Happy Birthday to Me for years.  A K-pop gem featured in the <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=q_ZUhJ33X80">SamBakZa flash animation "There she is!! Step 2 : Cake Dance."</a> </p>

<p>The song itself has an upbeat sounding melody with a story about the singers birthday coming up and wanting to spend it with a person they like, but who might not return the feelings (Another way to get into my 'Like' spectrum is to have a happy melody but sad / dark lyrics).</p>

<p>It may not be my birthday.  But every time it plays it feels like it is.</p>
<!-- wp:quote -->
<blockquote class="wp-block-quote"><p>It’s my birthday, honja-man-ui firstday<br>du-geun-dae-neun nan hwak dalla-jil geo-ya<br>neon moreu-gejji-man na dasi tae-eo-na<br>neo-e-ge-ro gal geo-ya<br>It’s my birthday, just me and my birthday<br>My heart is beating fast, I might change in a second<br>I don’t know about you, but if I were to be reborn<br>I would still run to you</p><cite>Bulldog Mansion - Happy Birthday to Me</cite></blockquote>
<!-- /wp:quote -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3><a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xgtdETevhUY" target="_blank">Princess Crocodile</a></h3>


<!-- wp:list -->
<ul><li> Artist(s): F.M. Einheit and Gry / Electro Swing / France</li></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->
<p>In an episode of a now defunct podcast called "The Sound Culture", a song called "Princess Crocodile" from an album called "White Mink Black Cotton" was played and since then, I have been in love with the Electro-Swing genre.</p>

<p>It sounds like an odd genre like Polynesian speed gospel or Melodic Power Speed Metal Rock (See 403 Forbiddena above).</p>

<p>Much like how anyone can make a 'good' movie out of anything, I believe the same can be done with music... but it's a little nuanced.  A movie can have a bad plot but amazing visuals that can warrant a repeat viewing.  Music, on the other hand, has just the sound with not much else to help it out, unless it's coupled with visuals to make a memorable experience (Once again, see 403 Forbiddena or Bulldog Mansion above both of which have fun flash animations associated with them).  </p>

<p>The album, "White Mink, Black Cotton" is a compilation of rare swing songs collected from various medias, remastered,  remixed by contemporary artists and given new life.  The instruments and beats added never overpower the original song and what seems like an experiment in sound turns into a great overall album.  </p>
<!-- wp:quote -->
<blockquote class="wp-block-quote"><p>Princess crocodile<br>A heart full of bells<br>Rings in your moves<br>Whatever you do, I see it all in you.</p><cite>Princess Crocodile&nbsp;- F.M. Einheit and Gry</cite></blockquote>
