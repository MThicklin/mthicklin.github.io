<p>I believe everyone should have a magic trick or two up their sleeve (Ba-dum-tish).  Nothing fancy.  Nothing they have to buy, have a hidden partner to help with, or require a lot of set up time.  Just a couple of sleight of hand tricks to pull out when the opportunity presents itself.</p>

<p>One of my favorites is the 'Back Palm Vanish' (BPV).  It's simple, requires next to no setup, and is versatile enough to be used with dollars, cards, pretty much anything you can hide behind your hand.</p>

<p>To start, take your item (a card or folded dollar), place the bottom edge on the back of your fingers.  Using your forefinger and pinkie, grip the 2 bottom corners (Picture 1).  When your hand is open, you're in the hidden position (Picture 2). When you close your hand, you're in the reveal position (Picture 3).  Be sure to note the corners between your fingers.  This is a quick, snappy trick so there is some leeway to having some corners poking out as your hand will be the main focus.  </p>

<p>  Hold your hand up, palm facing your audience and with a quick snapping motion, close your hand to reveal your item.</p>

<figure><img src="https://k3ntuckydesign.files.wordpress.com/2020/07/bpv-in-action.gif?w=576" alt="" class="wp-image-1500" width="278" height="494" /><figcaption><strong>Witch! Burn him at the stake!!</strong></figcaption></figure>

<p>This is important, KEEP YOUR HAND RELAXED. Your instinct maybe to tighten your grip on the item.  When you're closing your hand, this causes the item to move and shift out of your grip.</p>

<p>Ultimately,  When done the first time, you have surprise on your side so it will have a pretty good impact if people are paying attention.  But, this being such a simple trick, there are 2 things to keep in mind:  <br>1) It looks better when you snap the item out by quickly closing your hand.  If you do it slow, the audience will be able to see how it's done.<br>2) Doing the trick more then once will allow the audience to figure it out.  Everyone will say "Do it, again", so be prepared to either teach the trick or have the smart ones figure it out. </p>

<p>TL;DR</p>

<p>Hold your item (Card, folded dollar, etc) between your fore finger and your pinkie so the card is hidden when you hand is open.</p>

<p>Important: KEEP YOUR HAND RELAXED.  Your instinct will be to tighten your grip, doing so causes your item to shift out of your grip.</p>

<p>This is a fast trick, It looks better when done quickly.</p>

<p>This is a simple trick, doing it more then once will allow the audience to figure it out.</p>
