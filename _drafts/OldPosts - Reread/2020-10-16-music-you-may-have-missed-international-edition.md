**-OR- Other countries make music too?!**

It's weird coming across something popular in other countries or even worldwide but barely known in America.  One could surmise it's the cultural bubble one lives in with their country.  Others might cite America's tendency to shove entertainment down everyone's throats.  Maybe... Or maybe that's what you get to do when you're number #1!

So sit back, click a link, turn on subtitles and enjoy some foreign (At least to me) tunes!

**Stromae - Tous Les Mêmes**
["https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAMWdvo71ls]

In 2015, My wife and I made plans to travel around Europe.  While talking to my co-workers at the time, I said one of our stops would be Paris.  One co-worker stated they couldn't imagine going to a third-world country like France.  I was thrown off by the comment as I was under the impression France was a rather developed country (Dang near [aa.org/wiki/First_World]first world if I had to put a number to it).  She clarified that she considered them third world as they weren't as free as we are in America.  

Not really a *joke* or anything, More a *thing that happened and if I didn't get it out of my head I'm going to have an aneurysm*.  So, speaking of France...

[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAMWdvo71ls](Tous Les Mêmes, or All The Same - English title), is a French song from Belgian artist Stromae.  His name is a type of French slang where the syllables in a word are flipped,  Stromae is actually Maestro.  It's a popular troupe in French hip hop.  [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAMWdvo71ls](Tous Les Mêmes) is a song tells the story of a woman fed up with men and the way they act towards her.

The vocals and instrumentals work together really well.  Even if you don't French you can still enjoy this song. Like the top comment on the YouTube video says "I don't speak Croissant, but this song slaps!"

*Oi, what's all this then?  A Russian song?  Why does it sound so familiar?*

Not as much a "song", but a meme based on the instrumentals from Stromae's Tous Les Mêmes from a Russian animated story YouTube channel.  The song is about the youtuber going to pay taxes, seeing a friend on the street, telling the friend he's going to do business in a suit, his friend asking if he can live with him as he has no where to go and finally finishing about being knee deep in problems.

*That sounds nerdy, I'm leaving...*

Wait, Hold on...  Look, yes, it's nerdy but the channel is pretty decent if you like animated story channels like [a.com/channel/UCo8bcnLyZH8tBIH9V1mLgqQ](TheOdd1sOut) or [https://www.youtubeW8N-cghnLpA](Jaiden Animations). **Last Summer Whisper**
In the 1980's, a government project was developed to export American pop soft rock overseas to take over the world's radio stations.  Most rejected the sounds, but Japan was able to reverse engineer the sound and put their spin on it.  As proof, I present Japanese artist [a.com/watch?v=uWqnsVMc8CQ](ANRI's Last Summer Whisper).  

With soothing synth and soft rock style bass, it wouldn't be out of place on a commercial compiling the the best soft rock hits of the 80's.  You know the ones; a couple walking on the beach, a few shots of the waves crashing, 2 wine glasses toasting, a shot of the sunset.  Which makes sense when you find out the album this song is on, "Heaven Beach", was created to have the feel of [aa.org/wiki/Heaven_Beach](light American pop that makes you want to listen to it at the beach)".

**Rednex - Cotton Eyed Joe**
K3ntucky, Why is this here?  This is a country classic!  Born in the USA!

Oh, I'm just as surprised as you when I first found out the version of Cotton Eyed Joe that got pop music prominence back in 1994 was actually done by Swedish band Rednex.  

Still active, even though no new videos have been posted to their YouTube channel in 7 years, Rednex is an interesting mix of Eurodance and Bluegrass music.  The band members stage names reflect their red neck personas: Mary Joe (Annika Ljungberg), Bobby Sue (Kent Olander), Ken Tacky (Arne Arstrand), Billy Ray (Jonas Nilsson) and Mup (Patrick Edenberg).
