When I go out searching for new music, I try to tap different sources.  Many times it's the Youtube recommended list.  Sometimes... when I'm just absolutely desperate I try the radio.  On road trips it's definitely Sirius XM radio.

The road trip starts off with the the 80 - 2000's pop channels.  They are reliable for the first 2 days, but anything longer then that and you notice the repeats.  As the odometer ticks higher and I come to terms with the fact that they aren't going to make any more 80's - 2000's music.  I venture out for something new. Usually I land on a random channel and end up with something new (Imagine that, getting out of your comfort zone leads to exposure to new stuff).  

So sit back, buckle in and chill as I go over a couple of songs I've found on Sirius XM you may have missed.

<ul><li>Grimes & i_o</li></ul>

Despite the title making one assume heavier sounds (Much like the savage garden [pop group] / sound garden [heavier rock group] comparison), It's actually laid back.   Grimes isn't 'Grime' music; which I file next to 'Chopped and Screwed' as jokes that accidentally gained an audience.  She is "Synth Dream pop", a genre I rate a "Yeah" on the scale of "I get that".  Heavy smooth bass and smart rhythms combined with airy vocal creates a chill, slightly ominous atmosphere. 

>I am, like, begging for you babyMakes you wanna party, wanna wake upBaby, it's violence, violenceBaby, it's violence<cite>Grimes & i_o - Violence</cite></blockquote>

<h3 id="looking-like-this">["https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9GEmMx9R8io" ]Looking like this</a></h3>



<ul><li>Lyre Le Temps</li></ul>

In ["https://k3ntuckydesign.wordpress.com/2020/04/07/music-you-may-not-have-heard-of/" ]my last post music post</a>, I mentioned Electro Swing.  This would be a good introduction to the genre.  So many Electro swing songs tend to be remixes of, or borrow heavily from, big band / swing music to make something more contemporary.  This song falls into the latter category with a nice mix of horns and synths.  

>Am I looking like this?Tell me, am I looking like this?No not at all, you can't controlYou think I'm showy, but I'm not at all<cite>Lyre Le Temps - Looking like this</cite></blockquote>



<h3 id="lisa-frank-420-modern-computing">["https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bAgmGZ9iQ2Y" ]Lisa Frank 420 / Modern Computing</a></h3>



<ul><li>["https://open.spotify.com/artist/0aHFAt3c3C648WXJP7GCRc?autoplay=true" ]Macintosh Plus (Vektroid)</a></li></ul>

Nestled in the weirder parts of the internet is an interesting genre called vaporwave (not to be confuse with vaporware, software that is announced but never actually released).  The sound has been described as "made less for enjoyment than for the regulation of mood" and I feel that.  Equal parts internet meme,  music genre, and visual aesthetic; the album, Floral Shoppe, where Lisa Frank 420 / Modern Computing comes from is cited as the blueprint for the genre. The song isn't meant to be rocked out to but more of an ASMR vibe.  WARNING: Around the 5:20 mark, the instrumentals start getting out of sync with the vocals.  I'm sure this is a part of aesthetics, but it's annoys me to no end.

>I've made up my mind, time is running out, Make a moveOh, we can go on, Do you understand? It's all in your hands<cite>Lisa Frank 420 / Modern Computing - Macintosh Plus</cite></blockquote>



<h3 id="same-old">["https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vEi2wUcREJ4">Same Old</a></h3>



<ul><li>["https://iamoffbeat.com/" ]Offbeat</a></li></ul>

K3ntucky, is this more of that electro-swing crap you're always shoving in your reader's face?How dare you accurately describe what I'm doing!  But there is a twist dear reader, Offbeat isn't really so much Electro-Swing, he's more Swing-hop (Hip-swing?).  It's more combining hip-hop lyrics and swing type instrumentals.  Anyway, the genre shouldn't matter if it's a good song and I'm putting what little credibility I have left on the line to say that this is, indeed, a good song.  

It checks multiple boxes on the "fast track to my like list"... list:Innocent sounding song with dark lyrics, Check.  Tells a story, Check.  Nice smooth instrumentals, Check.  This song speaks to many people's fear of becoming complacence with life and not make being able to make it out of their small towns.  It's a fear for many people; For some it's hidden, for others it's a bit more.

>No matter where I’m going, no matter what I’m doingI can hear the sound of the town calling my nameIt just keeps going, it just keep pursuingCoz it knows if i’m falling it’s making its claim<cite>Offbeat - Same Old</cite></blockquote>
