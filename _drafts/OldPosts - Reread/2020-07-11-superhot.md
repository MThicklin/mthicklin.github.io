With Superhot: Control Mind Delete, coming out soon (Like July 16th 'soon'), I wanted to revisit one of my favorite games, the original Superhot.

In case you missed it, Superhot is a first person shooter where time only moves when you move.  (Which is a little bit of an exaggeration, time will still move, just incredibly slow, if your hands are off the mouse and keyboard, but you get the idea.  I'm not going to fault the game for that.)  That's it...  There are no health bars, no ammo drops, and one shot / one hit kills everybody including you.  Levels consist of white rooms and red guys, usually with guns, and your objective of killing them before they kill you.

With the 'time moving when you move' mechanic in play, a level could take a couple of minutes to beat.  Once you finish the level and see the play through in 'real' time. Making your run, little mistakes and all, look like they took the actor from John Wick and inserted him into a matrix fight scene.

The way Superhot delivers it's story is really interesting.  The basic plot starts out with your friend telling about this game they found.  They give you a copy and a crack to by-pass the game's security.  Once you start playing, things are easy enough.  You get used to the mechanics, you make a few cool looking play-throughs.  But you start receiving mysterious messages telling you to stop playing.  After another round of cracks from your friend you're back to playing, but things keep getting weirder and weirder. 

In addition to the regular plot, there is a meta plot in the background told through terminals hidden within the levels and a bit of text inside the EULA.  The terminals inside the levels add to the replay value once you  learn what's ultimately happening.  The text inside the EULA is a bit more specific and yet abstract at the same time.  Like a lot of good stories, it's open-ended enough to allow the player to make their own interpretations.  Are you being enslaved or are you being freed?  Is it this corporate warfare or the machinations of a rouge AI?  Are you playing the game or is the game playing you?

I do have a some gripes; a couple of very minor gripes but make no mistake, these are gripes that barely chip at the overall solid core of the game.  None of these are deal breakers but they are rather annoying when they come up:

Exiting the game takes a few seconds. This is a superficial thing to gripe about, but when you hit a cheap level and you want to rage quit, those seconds are excruciating.

I mentioned at the end of the level you see your play through in real time.  During this, you also have the ability to edit your video and upload it to Killstagram, Superhot's social media for sharing play-throughs.  But the user interface during explaining what to do is white with no outlines.  Combine that with the mostly white aesthetic of the game and you get just get white mess on the screen.

Let me tell  you about the level "Elevator Pitch"...  Let me tell you about the pain this level gives me... Because, dear reader, I don't claim to be a pro gamer.  I barely qualify as a amateur gamer (Maybe an immature gamer), but dagnabit (Pardon my language, but it needed to be said.) This level is my rage inducing level, everyone will find their own but this is mine.  You start in an elevator with 3 red guys with guns, We'll call them left, right, and center.  Center has his gun pointing at you but right will shoot first, so classic fake out.  you by the grace of luck you might to take them out, the elevator door opens and you're greeted by 2 red guys with close range shotguns.  

Next thing you know, your cursor is hovering over the uninstall button and your finger is hovering over the mouse button. 'No" you think to yourself, "You don't get off that easy, game."... Or maybe that's just me.

So, is Superhot good?  Dear reader, Superhot is DAMN good!  Superhotis the most innovative shooter I've played in years!  Don't get it twisted, you will run into levels that feel cheap.  You'll run into levels that you will get stuck on, dying over and over.  Too often you'll be so close to the end only to have a new red guy spawn and shoot you in the back.  But once you beat those levels...  In the ranking of life achievements, it's finding true love, birth of your children, beating those cheap levels of Superhot.

If you ["https://superhotgame.com/" ]buy Superhot</a> before July 16th you'll get the new game, Superhot control mind delete for free or you could wait and buy the bundle and give these people more money to keep making cool games.  Because if a game like Superhot is an indication of the level these developers are on, I think it's worth it.
